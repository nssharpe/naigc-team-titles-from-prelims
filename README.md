# Team Titles from Prelims

Rules analysis for NAIGC Nationals: what changes if the team competition is decided
on prelim scores and finals qualification becomes purely individual.

**Read it:** https://nssharpe.github.io/naigc-team-titles-from-prelims/

Modelled on the 2026 Nationals results, with the judging-drift question replicated
across 2024, 2025 and 2026.

Two modelling rules:

- finals sessions hold the **same number of routines** as in 2026 (routine-neutral);
- prelim team scores keep the **open-squad** definition (top 3 of everyone who competed).

"In a final" means having performed a scored routine — being named to a qualifying
team's squad without competing is not counted.
